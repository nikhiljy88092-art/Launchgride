Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for business owners.

Can I see a demo before paying for development?

Yes. Start with the Free Demo at ₹0. The Advance Demo is also available for ₹299 if you want more customization and a working preview.

What is included in Full App Development?

₹9,999 is the one-time Full App Development charge. It covers the agreed development scope, including the Customer App, Delivery App and Admin Panel/App. Additional features, integrations, services and optional plans are charged separately.

Is the Advance Demo compulsory?

No. It is optional. You can start with the Free Demo and decide whether you want the Advance Demo before development.

Is Play Store publishing included?

Play Store Publishing is an optional separate service for ₹6,000 one-time. Google Play Console subscription is separate where applicable.

Do you provide yearly support?

Yes. Annual Service plans are optional and charged separately after development: Basic ₹2,500 + GST/year and Advanced ₹4,999 + GST/year.

Can the app be customized for my business?

Yes. Business name, logo, branding, products/services and supported features can be customized according to your requirements.
